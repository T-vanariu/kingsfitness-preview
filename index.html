OUR STORY

Why Kings Fitness exists

“Lasting health requires more than guesswork.”

THE KINGS FITNESS PHILOSOPHY

Kings Fitness was founded in Port Moresby by coach Jordan King to help individuals and corporate teams build strength, lose weight, and prevent lifestyle diseases through structured, results-driven coaching.

Whether you are stepping into a gym for the first time or organizing an employee wellness initiative, we bring the expertise and accountability you need to succeed. Kings Fitness is proudly operated by ElevateX Limited.

Built for Papua New Guinea’s busy professionals. And for whoever you are right now.

“I’ve never been in a gym.”

The first-timer

You’ll never be thrown in the deep end. We start where you are, teach you the basics and make the gym feel like yours.

“I work 10-hour days.”

The busy professional

Before work, at lunch or after hours: short, efficient sessions that fit your calendar, not the other way round.

“My whole team needs this.”

The team leader

Organizing an employee wellness initiative? We run it end to end, from team workouts to NCD prevention workshops.
